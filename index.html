What to check before shipping

Match the exact option

Save the product URL, color, and size together. For clothing, compare garment measurements such as chest width and body length with an item you already own.

Review warehouse photos

If your agent provides quality-control (QC) photos, compare the received color and size label with your order. Ask for a close-up or a ruler photo if a detail matters to your decision.

Compare shipping quotes

Check quotes for the same destination and parcel dimensions. Record the billable weight, tracking, coverage, and any carrier restrictions beside each quote.

Estimate your delivered cost

Enter your own estimates in USD. Include any applicable duties or taxes in the last field.

Common questions

What is an ACBuy spreadsheet?

It is a shopping reference for keeping product links, variants, estimated prices, and notes together. This guide uses CAFANS listings for its catalog. Check the current product page to see which shopping agents it supports.

Where do the two main buttons go?

Both open the CAFANS homepage. From there you can find a listing and review the available purchase options on its product page.

Do the catalog prices include international shipping?

The catalog shows estimated item prices. Get a shipping quote for your destination and use the budget calculator to add shipping and other charges.
